# Low Key: upload what people already have

**Why (Dylan, 2026-11-04):** "there needs to be an option to upload a pic that was previously taken because that's the format that most people will have, a pic they have in library on phone or a pdf or pix in their email." Also: estimate the time from upload to the first response, and keep reads fast and cheap.

## Parts
1. "Choose a photo or PDF" (no `capture` attribute, so phones offer the library, Files and email attachments) next to "Take a photo"; drag and drop on desktop.
2. A PDF's first page rendered in the browser (pdf.js) to the same JPEG the reader gets; a calm message for a file the browser can't open.
3. An elapsed-time line while reading.
4. Shorter reads: the reader copies only lines that carry numbers, names or the dealer; guard against an empty read.
5. Measure the time to first response, cold and warm, on the real documents.
