## What the template / stack cannot do for the PRD (yet)
- **Speed:** 40 s for the first cards breaks the PRD's "under 15 seconds" bar, as in P10 (18–34 s). The scaffold streams progress text immediately, but the ranked cards arrive only after Spark's second reasoning round. Streaming card-by-card or a lower reasoning effort needs a follow-up decision.
- **Closing sentence:** Spark returned only progress sentences ("I'll pull ranked picks…") and no final plain-language summary in either live turn, despite an explicit prompt instruction. The cards carry every number, so the screen still answers "what to buy, what leaving costs", but the PRD's one-sentence "why this car" is not yet reliable from this model path.
- **Stub scope (by design for this task):** rankings, prices and stock are fixtures; the fuel forecast (P15/P16), live provider MCP search, and the weekly depreciation job are later tasks. The PRD's three-card text still contradicts its four-pick rule (carried from report 024); the app follows the four-pick rule (4 ranked + `badge`/`extra` commodity).
