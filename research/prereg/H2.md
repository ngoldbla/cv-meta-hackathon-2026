id: H2
claim: The predict-and-decide mixture (forecast pre-positioning + decision model + ESA fallback) beats ESA with schedule-forecast pre-positioning on held-out tail metrics.
primary_metric: p95 wait; secondary: cvar95 wait, peak_landing_density
comparisons: [mixture:<best dev checkpoint>, esa+schedule]; [mixture:<best dev checkpoint>, esa]
threshold: mean relative change in p95 <= -0.10, 95% CI excluding 0, Holm-adjusted Wilcoxon p < 0.05; cvar95 and peak_landing_density must not worsen by more than 5%
test: paired Wilcoxon signed-rank + bootstrap 95% CI over holdout instances; Holm across the three metrics
n_holdout_instances: 40
data: CAGES v0 (inversion regime), checkpoint chosen on dev only
models: the decision-model checkpoint and confidence threshold are fixed in a ledger entry before the holdout run
notes: The forecaster in the comparison arm is the same one the mixture uses, so the test isolates the decision model and fallback.
