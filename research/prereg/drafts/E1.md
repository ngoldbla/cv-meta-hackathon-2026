claim: In worst-case venue evacuations, what a rail operator can log (train departures and passenger counts, with fare gates open) understates the true mean evacuee-and-rider wait by at least 25%.
primary_metric: logger_understatement = 1 - mean(logged wait) / mean(true wait), logged wait = half the observed headway at the boarding platform, true wait = boarding time - arrival at the station
comparisons: none (single-arm estimate under the timetable controller and under express_rule)
threshold: holdout mean logger_understatement >= 0.25 with bootstrap 95% CI lower bound >= 0.25, for both controllers
test: bootstrap 95% CI over holdout instances
n_holdout_instances: 40
data: CAGES evacuation inversion regime on the 7 line (holdout split derived from CAGES_HOLDOUT_SALT); evaluator 2.0
models: none
notes: Passengers still waiting at the horizon are excluded from logged-vs-true pairs (they have no logged wait) and reported separately. Dev estimate (exploratory): 72.5% [69.2, 75.7] (research/results.md).
