A · One page, with sheets

Everything happens on the home page. Tapping a type fills the lineup in below the tiles, which shrink to a row of chips. A car, a listing, the settings and Check a quote each open as a bottom sheet over the lineup, and Back closes the top sheet. It stays closest to today's app and is the least work to build.

The numbers come from the live fast path (ZIP 30263, Sedan, 2026-11-04) and the app's quote code. The sample quote is an invented fixture.
