C · One comparison, opened in place

The results are a single comparison: every car's real cost on one scale, cheapest first. Tapping a car opens it in place, with its walk-away and its best listing, and closes any other that was open. The settings open inline under their summary line. Only Check a quote leaves the page. This concept is the densest: the whole decision is on one scroll.

The numbers come from the live fast path (ZIP 30263, Sedan, 2026-11-04) and the app's quote code. The sample quote is an invented fixture.
