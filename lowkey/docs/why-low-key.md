# Why Low Key works the way it does

Low Key is a low-key way to buy a car. This page holds the explanations that used to sit on the start screen (moved here in task 054, so the start screen is only the choice of area and type).

## How Low Key works
1. **Calm by design.** We can't fix the dealership. We can make the decision simple: a few good choices, side by side.
2. **Less risk.** Every car here is among the best at holding its value in its class, and every price includes the loan, gas and tax.
3. **Shop anonymously.** No account, no phone number. Dealers don't know you're looking until you contact them.
4. **Lowest listed prices.** We show the cheapest listing we find near you, how many others there are, and where its price sits among them, nearby and nationally.
5. **Details later.** No photos, colours or sales gimmicks until you're ready. Good car buying starts with a little distance.

## Why no photos?
Point 5 above: photos, colours and sales gimmicks wait until you're ready. Each car is drawn as a simple line figure of its type, so the choice stays about value, cost and the car's life, not its paint.

## What Low Key doesn't do
No ads · No sign-up · Not paid by dealers.
